## Owner approval checklist for each proposed message

Approve the exact destination, current draft text, sender identity, public links
and any requested contact details. The first approved contact should ask for
fit/permission when uncertain. Disclose maintainer involvement and Research Alpha
status; distinguish shared-library interoperability from independent scientific
validation. Link the fixed release, protocol, limitations and review PR. Explain
that five beta slots are planned, not filled, and that participation is voluntary.

Do not contact individuals from scraped lists, promise clinical utility or
incentives, claim users/downloads/testimonials, or repeat a declined invitation.
Any later response/decline is recorded as observed; silence is not interest.
No new release or outreach follows automatically from merging this PR.

## Measure genuine adoption without invented metrics

Maintain a manual, de-identified record of voluntarily submitted reproductions,
distinct external contributor PRs, retained failures and independently completed
beta tasks. State time window, build SHA and denominator. Do not count maintainer
or CI runs as independent users. Stars/downloads alone do not demonstrate use,
correctness or scientific impact. Quotes require separate permission. Start with
the [five-person beta protocol](../beta/PROGRAM.md), not a promotional claim.
