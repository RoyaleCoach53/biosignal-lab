# Code of conduct

BioSignal Lab welcomes participants across countries, disciplines, backgrounds,
and levels of experience. Treat people with respect and discuss ideas on their
merits. Make technical disagreement specific, constructive, and evidence-based.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with learners and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Respect differences in experience, language, and accessibility needs.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, give appropriate credit, and correct inaccurate claims.
- Protect private information and respect consent and data ownership.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, impersonation, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not
use project spaces to promote misleading medical claims or expose private data.

## Scope and reporting

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces used to represent this project. Reports can be sent privately to the
repository owner through a private contact method they publish on their GitHub
profile. A dedicated private conduct reporting address has not yet been
established. Do not post a sensitive report publicly to compensate for that gap.

Maintainers should review reports fairly, minimize disclosure, consider context,
and avoid conflicts of interest. Responses may include a correction request,
warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. A
participant may request reconsideration through the same private channel.

This is a project-specific policy; it is not presented as an adoption of an
external code-of-conduct template.
